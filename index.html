What we do

One practice for the quantitative questions behind big decisions

Six areas of work, one standard: an answer you can defend to a board, a regulator or a funder.

01

Experimentation and measurement

A/B tests, holdouts, geo experiments and marketing incrementality for products, ads and growth teams.

Did the feature lift retention? Which channels are truly incremental?

02

Pricing and market economics

Pricing strategy, demand and elasticity estimation, market sizing and competitive analysis.

What should we charge? How big is the opportunity, really?

03

Forecasting and decision intelligence

Forecasts, scenario and risk models, and decision-support tools for financial institutions, regulators and operating teams.

What happens to our exposure if conditions shift? Where should we look first?

04

Impact evaluation and value for money

Evaluation design, power calculations, cost-effectiveness and value-for-money analysis for programmes, investments and policies.

Did the programme work? What does each result cost?

05

Economic statistics and data systems

Indicator frameworks, productivity and labour measurement, survey and sampling design, data-quality reviews and dashboards.

Are we measuring the right thing, and can we trust the data?

06

AI, data and policy

Measuring the economic and social effects of AI and digital technology, and building AI-assisted analytics workflows that make teams faster.

How is AI changing work and markets? How do we use it well?

About

Muthoni Ngatia

Founder and Principal, Acutis Metrics

Muthoni is an applied economist and data scientist whose work spans experimentation, pricing, forecasting, impact evaluation and economic measurement. At Microsoft and Amazon she has led economics and data science work for products and businesses at scale, including Amazon Prime and AWS.

Before industry, she was a Young Professional and then an Economist at the World Bank, where she designed and ran impact evaluations across Madagascar, Malawi, Rwanda, Nigeria, Guinea, Kenya, Senegal, Burkina Faso, Chad, Comoros and India.

She holds a PhD in Economics from Yale. She is from Nairobi, is based in Washington, DC, and works in English, French and Kiswahili.
